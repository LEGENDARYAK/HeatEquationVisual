What is being solved

With α = κΔt/Δx², FTCS updates T by T + α·(second difference) and is only stable for α ≤ ½. Fully implicit solves (I − αD)Tⁿ⁺¹ = Tⁿ at every step. Crank–Nicolson averages the two: (I − αD/2)Tⁿ⁺¹ = (I + αD/2)Tⁿ. The implicit solves use the Thomas algorithm for tridiagonal systems, plus a Sherman–Morrison correction for the periodic ring.

The analytic curve expands the starting temperature in the eigenmodes of the chosen boundaries (sines, cosines, or both) and decays each one by exp(−κλ²t). Built from PHYS 332 Homework 03 by Akul Khanna.
